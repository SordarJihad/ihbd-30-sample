# Order Khata

## Problem
Small Facebook page sellers take orders in Messenger comments and inbox, then copy them by hand into a notebook or Excel every night. Orders get missed, phone numbers are copied wrong, and they don't know which cash-on-delivery parcels are still unpaid. Last week a saree seller I know lost three orders because the messages were buried under 200 new chats, and she spent more than an hour each night making the courier list.

## Who
Facebook page clothing and saree sellers in Dhaka who run the page alone or with one helper and get roughly 20 to 50 orders a day, mostly cash on delivery through Pathao or Steadfast courier.

## Today's solution
A paper khata, sometimes a Google Sheet, screenshots of chats saved in the phone gallery, and memory. Some pay a part-time helper to copy orders. Big tools exist but they are made for shops with websites, cost a lot and are in English.

## Why me
My aunt runs exactly this kind of page and I help her on weekends, so I can watch the problem every week and talk to her seller friends. I can build simple web apps with Next.js and I will use AI tools to move faster.

## One-line pitch
Order Khata helps Facebook page sellers log every Messenger order in ten seconds and see unpaid COD parcels at a glance, instead of a messy notebook.
