# Interviews

## Interview 1
Date: 2026-11-27
Role: Saree seller, Facebook page, Mirpur, ~30 orders/day
Pain: 5
Quote: "Every night I sit with the khata for an hour to make the Steadfast list. Last Friday I forgot two orders."
Notes: Uses paper khata + phone gallery screenshots.

## Interview 2
Date: 2026-11-27
Role: Kids clothing seller, page run with sister, Uttara, ~20 orders/day
Pain: 4
Quote: "The inbox moves so fast, I lose who asked for which size."
Notes: Tried a Google Sheet, gave up after a week because typing on phone is slow.

## Interview 3
Date: 2026-11-28
Role: Three-piece seller, ~45 orders/day, has one helper
Pain: 4
Quote: "My helper copies orders, but COD money from courier never matches what we wrote."
Notes: Biggest pain is reconciling courier payments.

## Interview 4
Date: 2026-11-28
Role: Cosmetics reseller, home-based, ~15 orders/day
Pain: 2
Quote: "I manage in my head mostly, it's fine unless it's Eid."
Notes: Pain only spikes in festival season.

## Interview 5
Date: 2026-11-29
Role: Hijab and abaya seller, Chattogram, ~25 orders/day, sells only through Facebook live
Pain: 5
Quote: "During a live I get 60 comments in 10 minutes. After the live I scroll back and still miss people."
Notes: Writes orders on a whiteboard during the live, then photographs it.

## What I learned
Missed orders and the nightly courier list are the strongest pains (4 of 5). COD reconciliation came up twice, so I will keep it in "Not now" for the MVP but note it. Low-volume sellers (interview 4) are not my first users.
